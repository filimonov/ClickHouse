The GC credited the first sighting of a mount token with a clock sample taken before the `LIST` of `gc/server-roots/` and the slot read. A slow walk to the slot counted as time the token had been watched, and a later round could write `gc_fenced` over a holder whose last renewal still gave it authority. The sighting is now dated after the read that returned the token.

**Why it matters for the lease**

The GC on another server is the observer that decides a writer is dead: it waits until the slot token has stayed unchanged for `cas_mount_lease_ttl_ms + 5 % + its own poll step` on its own clock (about 41.5 s with the defaults), then sets `gc_fenced` and drops the protection of that writer's unfinished work. It never compares clocks across servers; the only thing that makes the decision safe is that the wait starts when the observer actually saw the token. Starting it earlier shortens the window below the lease, so a holder that still has authority can be fenced: a ref-log append that lands after such a fence leaves a reference to blobs the GC may delete.

This becomes reachable with the next change of the series (https://github.com/Altinity/ClickHouse/issues/2431): the background renewal will keep retrying through a storage outage instead of fencing itself after 4 s, so a live holder will routinely spend tens of seconds without changing its token. Roll this change out on GC nodes before that one reaches the holders.

**Changes**
- `computeHeartbeatFloor` takes the observer clock as a function; both callers (the regular round and the rebuild) pass it. The first sighting is sampled after the read; the re-decision after a refused fence-out dates its observation anew.
- The stability test keeps its entry sample, and a sample older than the sighting never counts as time held (`MountIncarnationObservation::heldFor`), so the elapsed time cannot wrap.
- A transient read error leaves the accumulated observation untouched (behaviour kept, now pinned by a test).

**Risks / notes**
- GC-side only, in-memory state; no format or protocol change. Mixed versions are safe in either order for correctness, but the deployment order above is needed before the renewal change.
- The rebuild path cannot fence in production (it runs a one-shot `Gc` with an empty observation map); the fix there keeps both callers consistent.

**Testing**
- `CaMountObserveCore` rows `sab_sighting_before_read` and `sab_redecision_stale_sample` show the fence inside authority without the fix; `main` is green with it.
- Unit: five regression tests red on the base (`gtest_cas_mount.cpp`, `gtest_cas_gc_ack_floor.cpp`: round and rebuild), two behaviour-preservation tests; `CAS*` gate 2615 (release), 2619 (ASan, no error).

Closes backlog task CAS-342.
