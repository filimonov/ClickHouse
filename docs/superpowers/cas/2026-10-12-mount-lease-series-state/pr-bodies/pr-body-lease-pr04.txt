While a CAS mount lease renewal retries, `system.cas_mounts` showed nothing: an aging `last_renewed_at`, `lifecycle` still `Live`, and the attempt counters moved only after the call ended. An operator could not tell a renewal retrying through an object-store outage from a renewal thread that had died. Three columns now report the renewal as it runs.

**Why it matters for the lease**

The next change of the series (https://github.com/Altinity/ClickHouse/issues/2431) makes the renewal retry until the store gives a definitive answer, so a live holder will spend tens of seconds — or longer — inside one renewal call. Without these columns that pause has no visible sign of life; with them, `SELECT lifecycle, renewal_in_progress, renewal_attempts, last_renewal_error FROM system.cas_mounts` during an outage reads `Live / 1 / 7 / "put gc/server-roots/…/mount: Timeout …"`. The same fields were the missing evidence in the incident analysis of #2431.

**Changes**
- `system.cas_mounts`: `renewal_in_progress` (a renewal call is running), `renewal_attempts` (physical `PUT`s sent by the current call), `last_renewal_error` (verb, key and error class of the last refused request; no credentials, no bodies). Appended at the end; existing queries are unaffected. Documented in `docs/en/operations/system-tables/cas_mounts.md`.
- Request engine: a `RequestObserver` with `on_write_attempt` and `on_failure` callbacks, called per sent attempt and per refused request.
- Renewal thread: a `RenewalProgress` record under a leaf mutex (`renewal_progress_mutex`, never held across I/O or allocation beyond one string move); `in_progress` is reset by `SCOPE_EXIT` on every exit, exceptions included. `CASMountRenewalAttempts`/`CASMountRenewalRetries` are counted per attempt instead of from the result.
- A local failure of the resolve read (not a request to the store) is not published.

**Risks / notes**
- Nothing changes on the store or in the protocol. Cost: one leaf-mutex lock per attempt, at one renewal per `cas_mount_renew_period_ms`.
- The system-table read copies the record under the lock and releases it before its own `LIST` of the slots.

**Testing**
- Unit: the engine observer receives attempts and failures; `renewalProgress` follows the call and resets on an exception; the local-failure filter (red on the base: `gtest_cas_request_observer.cpp`, `gtest_cas_mount_renewal_progress.cpp`).
- Integration: `test_cas_mount_renewal_retry` gains a scenario that reads the columns while RustFS is stopped.
- `CAS*` gate: 2614 (release), 2618 (ASan, no error).

Related: https://github.com/Altinity/ClickHouse/issues/2431
