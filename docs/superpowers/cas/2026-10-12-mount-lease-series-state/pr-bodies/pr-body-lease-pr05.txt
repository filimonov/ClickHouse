`probeSentinel` threw "gave up at the policy deadline" when the jittered pause before a reissue ended exactly on the policy deadline, although a probe had already run and its outcome was known. The loop now keeps the last probe outcome and returns it when the deadline is reached after a probe.

**Why it matters**

The sentinel probe is the typed, cache-bypassing read of the keys whose presence is an authoritative fact about the pool (`_pool_meta`, the owner anchor, the mount slot). It never conflates a transport failure with absence: `Indeterminate` means "nothing is known", and every consumer treats it fail-closed. Turning a known `Indeterminate` into a give-up exception on the boundary loses that information: the caller cannot tell "no probe ran" from "a probe ran and was inconclusive". The pre-sleep check accepts a pause that ends exactly on the deadline (one millisecond out of the 0–5000 ms jitter draw), so about one reissue in five thousand hit the loop-head give-up instead — the cause of the rare failures of `CASSentinelProbe.NativeClassifiesUnmodeledErrorAsIndeterminate`.

**Changes**
- `CasOperation::probeSentinel` keeps `last_probe`; at the loop head, a reached deadline returns it when a probe has run and throws `giveUpReadDeadline` as before when none has.
- The admission gate (a lost fence, no lease budget) still throws at any attempt; no request is sent after the deadline.
- The header comment states the admission rule as the code applies it.

**Testing**
- Regression test with an injected clock and a zero reservation: the pause lands exactly on the deadline; the outcome is `Indeterminate`, no request is sent after the deadline. Red on the base: `gave up at the policy deadline after 1 attempt(s)`.
- Two behaviour-preservation tests: a refusal after a pause on the deadline still throws; a deadline with no probe still throws.
- `CASSentinelProbe.NativeClassifiesUnmodeledErrorAsIndeterminate`: 100,000 repeats, 0 failures.
- `CAS*` gate: 2611 (release), 2615 (ASan, no error).

Closes backlog task CAS-325.
