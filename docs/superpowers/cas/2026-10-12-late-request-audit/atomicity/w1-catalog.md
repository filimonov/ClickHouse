**Top findings**

1. **VERIFIED-IN-CODE — body/marker race defeats the relink model’s source-liveness premise.**  
   Writer HEAD sees body; GC deletes body and marker; writer GET sees no marker and adopts (`ensureBlobPresent`, 403–440).  
   A resulting source ref can later confirm successfully over missing bytes. Importing this sibling trace breaks `ConfirmedRelinkNeverDangles`. Read marker before the final body HEAD, rejecting absence/token change.

2. **INFERRED — late `Removing` can reopen a safety-sensitive admission window.**  
   `dropNamespaceImpl` times out; fresh GET still sees `Live`; it reopens admission; old CAS subsequently lands.  
   The sequence and reopening branch are verified in code. Admission inconsistency follows; accepted-write loss requires additional terminal/handoff interleaving and was not established here.

3. **INFERRED — authority restoration excludes outstanding destructive requests.**  
   Old GC retains catalog erase; authority becomes unreadable; rebuild adopts incompatible proof; old erase lands on unchanged catalog token.  
   `LoseAuthority` excludes this trace by construction. Allowing it with late landing should fail `DeleteUsesCurrentAdoptedProof`; healthy takeover remains token/drain protected.
