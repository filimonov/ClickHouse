**Top findings**

1. **Premature fencing with mixed leases — VERIFIED-IN-CODE.**  
   Holder TTL 180 s; observer TTL 60 s, cadence 10 s: code fences an unchanged token after 73 s.  
   Holder authority can persist until 180 s. Model instead waits the longer lease, hiding `NoFenceInsideAuthority` failure. Both base and lease commits have this discrepancy.

2. **Admission does not fence later durable effects — VERIFIED-IN-CODE; data-loss consequence INFERRED.**  
   Writer passes `gate`; execution/transport stalls; GC fences or successor remounts; old request then lands.  
   `Client::doRequest` also resends after credential/redirect answers despite `SingleAttempt`. Conditional ref-slot seals protect their keys; a general mutation-after-fence guarantee is unproved.

3. **Saved responses are replaced by current-store oracles — VERIFIED-IN-CODE.**  
   Renewal lands or GET observes exact bytes; GC fences/successor replaces; delayed confirmation consumes the saved result.  
   Retry-model acceptance disables this transition. Correct equal-lease timing may keep authority closed, but the claimed response correspondence is incomplete.

4. **Successful slot occupancy is fused with acknowledgment — VERIFIED-IN-CODE.**  
   Retry observes its bytes; checkpoint publication becomes ambiguous or generation moves before installation.  
   Code withholds acknowledgment; model acknowledges immediately. Extend the invariant beyond historical object durability to confirmed frontier publication and live admission.
